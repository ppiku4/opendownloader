OpenDownloader
==============

ADD THE BROWSER EXTENSION (one time)

  Chrome / Edge / Brave / Opera / Vivaldi
    1. Open chrome://extensions  (Edge: edge://extensions)
    2. Turn on "Developer mode"
    3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the "extension\chromium" folder
       inside the OpenDownloader folder. (Start menu > OpenDownloader >
       "Browser extension" opens that folder.)

  Firefox
    about:debugging  >  This Firefox  >  Load Temporary Add-on
    >  choose extension\firefox\manifest.json

The extension icon shows a green dot when it is connected to the app.

TORRENTS
  Click a magnet link in your browser, open a .torrent file, or use
  Tasks > Open torrent file... . Seeding settings: Options > Torrents.

HELPER TOOLS
  ffmpeg (joins separate video + audio) and yt-dlp (videos on pages such as
  YouTube) are offered for download the first time they are needed.
  Manage them in Options > Video.

OpenDownloader.exe is also the browser bridge: when your browser starts it,
it runs invisibly and passes downloads to the app.
